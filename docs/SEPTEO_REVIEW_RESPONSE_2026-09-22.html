1 What the 22 comments add up to

They are not cosmetic. Taken together they describe a different deal: a wholesale data purchase at fixed prices, with permanent storage and reuse across STP’s own client base, no group mechanics, no non-circumvention, and Germany and Austria carved out. Our draft is a per-case reseller agreement. That gap is the meeting.

2 The one question to settle first

Is a delivered Document a per-case delivery, or a permanent entry in STP’s own database?

Comments #20 and #13 are not two points, they are one. Fixed prices are compatible with per-case delivery, not with permanent multi-client reuse. We pay our sources per retrieval; if STP stores a document once and serves many clients from it, our cost per delivered answer keeps running while our revenue stops. Any fixed price built on that assumption is wrong within months.

Settle this single point first. The minimum, the term, the liability cap and §6 all fall into place behind it — and each of them is negotiable once it is decided.

Their own comment gives us the lever

On §2 Subject matter their counsel writes: „Registerinformationen auf Anfrage (Einzelfallbezogen)“ (#4) — register information on request, case by case. That is our model, written by their lawyer, four clauses before the comments that ask for permanent storage and multiple use. Put both on the table and ask them which one they meant.

4 Point by point

Each item: what they wrote, what it means, the position we open with, and the fallback we can concede without breaking the model.

A · Fixed prices instead of the revenue share

#20, #21 · §8, §8.2
They wrote
„Anderes Pricing-Modell gewünscht. Feste definierte Preise notwendig.“ · „Feste Preise definieren.“
Means
Drop the 50 % net-amount share; give them a per-case wholesale price they can calculate against, and let them set their own end-client price.
Position
Accept — and say so early, it is the cheapest goodwill in the call. Our purchase, sale and margin are already open to them from the deck, so a fixed rate card costs us no information we have not already given. One condition: a fixed price is a price per Case — one company, one jurisdiction, one End Client. That is the sentence that must be said in the same breath.
Fallback
Rate card per band with volume tiers, reviewed annually, instead of a share — the same money, a shape their procurement understands.

B · Permanent storage and reuse for several clients

#6, #7, #9, #13 · §3.1, §3.5, §5.1
They wrote
„Auch eine dauerhafte Speicherung im eigenen Datenbestand und Verarbeitung für verschiedene Kunden“ · „Nicht nur End Clients“ · „Keine zeitliche Begrenzung der Nutzungsrechte“ · „Mehrfachnutzung muss zulässig sein“
Means
They want to build an internal register out of what they buy: store it permanently, serve it to any number of their clients, and use it for their own purposes beyond the End Client relationship.
Position
Not for free — and this is the only point worth risking the meeting over. We buy per retrieval. A stored copy serving a second client is a second delivery that nobody paid for. What we can grant today, and should offer in the same sentence so the „no“ is not empty: (i) unlimited retention of a delivered Document by the End Client it was ordered for — #9 is almost entirely satisfiable, the time limit can go; (ii) STP’s own archival, audit, backup and support copy, and free re-delivery to the same End Client, without limit; (iii) free reuse inside a freshness window for the same company and jurisdiction, which our platform already implements — and the window is a commercial dial we can turn up.
Fallback
If they insist on the pool: it becomes a priced licence, not a permission — a reuse fee per document served to a further client, or an annual pool licence per jurisdiction. Never a free clause.

C · The minimum, recalculated

#22 · §8.4
They wrote
„Summe des insgesamt von STP gezahlten Kaufpreises ./. Procurement Costs = Founders1’s Gewinn p.m. Wenn Gewinn > 1.500 => keine über den vereinbarten regulären Kaufpreis geschuldete Zahlung, wenn Gewinn < 1.500 => Ausgleich der Differenz“
Means
They accept a minimum and have written the top-up mechanic themselves — measured on our margin, not on turnover. It is the construction we wanted.
Position
Accept the formula. Two points to correct: it drops the 750 € ramp for months 1–3 we had already conceded — take the ramp back to full 1.500 € from month 1, or keep the ramp and trade it against the 6-month term (item J). And note what their own formula requires: it only computes if they see our procurement cost, so Annex 2.1 transparency survives on their own proposal, not ours.
Fallback
If they would rather not see the cost at all: minimum becomes a flat monthly fee on our margin with no cost disclosure. Simpler for us, and we keep the number.

D · Cost changes

#23 · §8.6
They wrote
Only where costs have actually risen · define a maximum increase · price then fixed for at least x months · „Was ist, wenn die Bezugskosten niedriger werden, besteht dann eine Verpflichtung zur Anpassung nach unten?“
Means
Four reasonable asks, and the fourth is the real one: symmetry.
Position
Give all four. Evidenced increases only; a cap per jurisdiction per year; 12 months’ stability after any change; and yes to passing decreases on, reviewed quarterly. It costs us nothing we control and it is the answer that makes the rest of §8 look fair.

F · Delete §6 non-circumvention entirely

#16 · §6
They wrote
„Ziffer 6 insgesamt streichen“
Means
Free rein to approach the suppliers and routes they learn about from us — while Annex 2 hands them the coverage map and the cost per jurisdiction.
Position
No, and the argument is not defensive: §6 is what makes our transparency possible. Annex 2 tells them exactly which jurisdictions we cover and at what cost. That disclosure is the know-how; §6 is its price. Delete §6 and the procurement costs go back out of the contract into a separate confidential price list — which is where they were until 15 September.
Fallback
Shorten the tail from 24 to 12 months, and state expressly that it binds only information obtained from us — which the clause already says, so the concession is real to them and cheap to us.

G · Delete §4 Septeo group scope

#11 · §4
They wrote
„streichen“
Means
Ambiguous, and the difference is large: either no group use at all (STP only), or group use without the accession mechanics (i.e. free extension to any Septeo company).
Position
Ask before answering. If it is the first, delete it gladly — a STP-only contract is narrower, stays German–German, and is what we preferred anyway. If it is the second, §4.2 and §4.3 must survive in some form: STP as sole contracting and billing party, and liable for its affiliates. Without those two, „Septeo group“ is an undefined and unbilled set of users.
Fallback
Delete §4 and add one sentence: any further Septeo entity joins by written amendment.

H · §5.2 non-compete — „explain what is excluded“

#14 · §5.2
They wrote
„Bitte erklären, was ausgeschlossen werden soll“
Means
They fear the clause reaches their existing register business. That is a real fear and it is not what the clause does.
Position
Answer plainly, then offer the carve-out they actually need. The clause bars using our coverage classification, availability information and document-kind mapping to build a rival aggregation product. It does not touch their existing business, their own sourcing, public information, or general know-how — §5.2a already says the last three. Offer to name their existing register business in an express carve-out. It costs nothing and removes the objection.
Fallback

I · No pass-through to their end clients

#25 · §9, Annex 5
They wrote
„Nicht gewünscht. STP soll in der Bestimmung der Vertragsbeziehung zu den eigenen Kunden frei sein.“
Means
They will not have us write into their client terms. Understandable — and our clause already said they may use their own wording, so part of this is a misread we can clear up.
Position
Concede the form, keep the substance. Drop Annex 5 as a document. Replace it with one clause naming three obligations that must be reflected in substance: no onward supply of the Documents as a service to the End Client’s own customers; no dataset resale or bulk extraction; use limited to the End Client’s own compliance and file purposes. Wording, placement and disclosure entirely theirs.
Fallback
Two of the three, if the third is genuinely unworkable — but never the first.

J · Warranty — they want more

#29, #30, #31 · §12
They wrote
„der gelieferte Inhalt der Dokumente muss dem Inhalt des betreffenden Registers im Zeitpunkt der Abfrage durch Founders1 oder den beauftragten Dritten entsprechen“ · „nur den korrekten Abruf der dort veröffentlichten Daten und die diesbezügliche Auslieferung an STP“
Means
They want the warranty to cover correct retrieval and faithful delivery — and their own #30 confirms we do not warrant the register’s own correctness.
Position
Accept in substance — this is what we already do. Redraft §12.1: each Document corresponds to the content of the relevant register as published at the time of retrieval by us or by a third party we commission, and is delivered unaltered with accurate provenance. Keep §12.2 as it stands; their comment endorses it. Remedy stays re-procurement at no charge or a credit.
Fallback
—

K · Liability cap rejected

#33 · §13.2
They wrote
„Nein. So nicht gewünscht.“
Means
Nothing, yet. There is no counter-proposal — it is a blank rejection, so whoever names a number first sets the frame.
Position
Make them name it. Then ours: cap at the fees paid in the preceding twelve months — a cleaner base than „our share“ once prices are fixed — with procurement pass-through excluded and the usual carve-outs (intent, gross negligence, personal injury, guarantees). A cap tied to a small annual fee is normal for a service at this price; an uncapped one is not available at this price, and that sentence is the whole argument.
Fallback
Higher multiple of annual fees, or a separate higher cap for data-protection breaches.

L · Six-month test phase

#35 · §14.1
They wrote
„Testphase von zunächst 6 Monaten.“
Means
No 24-month commitment. Read against #20 and #22 it is the classic combination: short term, best prices, no volume promise.
Position
Tradeable, never free. Offer: six-month initial term that rolls into 24 months unless terminated at the end of month 6. Price protection and the minimum follow the term, not the other way round. Say it in one line: a pilot, fixed prices and no commitment — pick two.
Fallback
Six months with no minimum at all, at list prices without the reseller discount.

M · Loss of supply — define the cases

#36 · §14.2a
They wrote
„Definieren, welche Fälle gemeint sind: Schließung des entsprechenden Registers? Abkündigung des Vertrages mit einem Drittdienstleister?“
Means
Fair question about a clause we wrote loosely on purpose.
Position
Define it. Two triggers: (i) the register closes, stops publishing, or publication becomes legally barred; (ii) a supplier terminates or loses its own access and no replacement route is available at comparable cost and quality within 60 days. Both evidenced, with a good-faith duty to seek a replacement first.

#8, #37 · §3.2, §3.3, §14.4
They wrote
„Zu besprechen.“ (API licence and own brand) · „Bitte erläutern“ (what survives termination)
Means
Nothing yet. Both are questions, not positions.

Ask what they actually intend to build — the answer also tells us how serious item B is. On survival: restrictions, non-circumvention, confidentiality, data protection and liability survive; §5.2 and §6.1 for their stated periods; delivered Documents stay usable. 5 Take these four questions into the call

  1. Case-by-case or pool? Their §2 comment says einzelfallbezogen, their §3 and §5 comments say permanent storage for several clients. Which is it — because the price model follows from the answer, not the other way round.
  2. §4 deleted — STP alone, or the whole group without mechanics?
  3. What liability figure do they want? They rejected ours without naming one.
  4. What do they want to build on the API? („Zu besprechen“ on §3.2/3.3.)

6 Two things not to do

Do not concede item B in the room

Permanent storage with multi-client reuse is the one point that changes what we are selling, and it collides with the supply terms upstream of us. If they press, the answer is „that is a different licence and it has a price“, not yes and not no. Nothing else in these 22 comments is worth the same firmness.

Keep the record of what is negotiated

Counsel’s warning on the draft applies now: if this is read as AGB, the liability cap, the warranty carve-out and the restrictions in §5 are the clauses at risk under § 307 BGB — and light resistance is not agreement. Minute the call: which clauses were discussed, what each side proposed, and what was conceded in exchange for what. A short written summary sent the same day is worth more than the clause wording it records.
